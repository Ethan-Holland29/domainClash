# JJK visual direction

Requested direction: make the user's real camera the centerpiece and launch techniques from the user's actual hands. Replace the entire avatar arena.

References looked up:
- [Piercing Blood](https://jujutsu-kaisen.fandom.com/wiki/Piercing_Blood): compressed blood, a concentrated source and a narrow long-range beam.
- [Blood Manipulation](https://jujutsu-kaisen.fandom.com/wiki/Blood_Manipulation): convergence into a dense orb before discharge.
- [Chimera Shadow Garden](https://jujutsu-kaisen.fandom.com/wiki/Chimera_Shadow_Garden): liquid darkness filling the environment and emerging shadow forms.
- [Shadow Garden anime sequence reference](https://makeagif.com/gif/chimera-shadow-garden-jujutsu-kaisen-2dj4o-): dark, high-contrast environmental transformation.

Implementation decisions:
- Cleave: pale, razor-like fan slashes with dark inner edges, short-lived glow and impact fragments, launched from the palm.
- Piercing Blood: red/black liquid spirals at the index tip, glossy compressed core, white-hot narrow beam, crimson helix and elliptical shock rings.
- Shadow Garden: expanding void from both hands, layered black liquid, cool reflections and tapered ink tendrils. The user remains visible through the center.
- Persistent cursed energy: fine blue electrical arcs and faint rings around hands, without obscuring fingers.
- Minimal studio frame and technique cards; no character figures or tiny camera inset.

All visual effects are procedural animations composited with live video, rather than pasted anime screenshots.

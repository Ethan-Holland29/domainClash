# Domain research and implementation notes

Research date: September 26, 2026. Pose descriptions use the user-supplied hand-sign chart, cross-checked against web descriptions and imagery. The chart is bundled at `public/hand-sign-reference.jpg`. It also depicts Time Cell Moon Palace, which was not requested and is not implemented.

The intricate interlacing in these drawings is not fully observable with a two-hand landmark tracker. The guide explicitly identifies camera approximations. These are silhouette recognizers, not trained classifiers or proof of canonical finger topology. All gestures are now armed regardless of the visible card page. More-specific domain silhouettes outrank overlapping simpler poses. All moves can also be cast from their cards or keys 1–3, anchored to visible hands in camera mode.

Supplementary hand-sign research: [hand-sign symbolism](https://animehunch.com/jujutsu-kaisen-domain-expansion-hand-signs-meaning-symbolism-explained/), [Yuji's hand sign](https://animehunch.com/yuji-itadoris-domain-expansion-hand-sign/), [Yuji discussion](https://comicbook.com/anime/news/jujutsu-kaisen-yuji-domain-expansion-hand-sign/). Symbolism articles do not establish every hidden finger position; the supplied drawing remains the visual reference for those details.

## Cleave

- Hand arrangement: Spread all five fingers with your palm facing the camera.
- Visual adaptation: A fan of luminous cutting blades releases from your palm.
- Original synthesized sound: Sharp double slash.
- Gameplay: 1s cooldown from activation.
- Qualification: Webcam shortcut for the cutting technique.

## Piercing Blood

- Hand arrangement: Point your index finger; fold your ring and little fingers. Aim the fingertip toward the target.
- Visual adaptation: Concentrated blood spirals into a crimson core before firing a narrow beam.
- Original synthesized sound: Rising liquid-pressure buzz.
- Gameplay: 1s cooldown from activation.
- Qualification: Webcam shortcut; the series uses two-handed compression.

## Chimera Shadow Garden

- Hand arrangement: Clasp both hands tightly with fingers interlaced; bring the curled fingers together in front of your chest.
- Visual adaptation: A liquid shadow floor spreads outward, with dark tendrils and ripples.
- Original synthesized sound: Deep ascending shadow chord.
- Gameplay: 14s cooldown from activation, 2.4s opening and 9s active environment.
- Qualification: Keep both wrists visible; detection approximates the clasp with two close fists.
- Description reference: [Chimera Shadow Garden](https://jujutsu-kaisen.fandom.com/wiki/Chimera_Shadow_Garden).

## Idle Death Gamble

## Canon and gameplay boundaries

Ryu and Uro's domain names and effects remain unrevealed in the referenced descriptions. Their effects here are labeled interpretations of Granite Blast and Sky Manipulation. Yuji's domain remains unnamed. Womb Profusion is the common English title; the user's Womb Perfusion spelling is preserved in the guide note. Death Gamble is listed under Idle Death Gamble.

The game interprets each environment visually; it does not reproduce all canonical sure-hit mechanics. All domains share the existing duel damage multiplier and periodic damage rules. Hakari's canonical jackpot duration is not used as a four-minute gameplay lock. There is no real-money gambling. Audio motifs are generated locally using oscillators and noise envelopes.

The camera remains the main compositing surface. Environments reveal from the captured hand anchor and settle around the frame. No body segmentation, room reconstruction, or physical occlusion is claimed.
